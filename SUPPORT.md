# Support

This is a community project without a support team. Here is how to get help quickly.

1. **In the application:** *Health → Run system check*. Most problems come with an explanation
   and, where possible, a *Fix automatically* button.
2. **Read:** [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md) · [FAQ](docs/faq.md) ·
   [Supported platforms](docs/supported-platforms.md) · [Known beta limitations](docs/beta.md)
3. **Ask or report:** [open an issue](https://github.com/ambartsumov/unified-workstation/issues/new/choose)
   and choose the form that fits — bug, installation, platform compatibility, feature.
   Attach a support bundle (*Health → Export support bundle*); it is redacted, and you can read
   it before sending.
4. **Security problems:** [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — privately, not as an issue.

Please do not post passwords, keys, tokens or private addresses anywhere, and do not send your
work files: they are never needed.

Problems inside Syncthing, Deskflow or Tailscale themselves are best reported to those
projects; if this product configures them wrongly, that is ours.
