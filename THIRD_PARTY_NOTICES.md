| Component | Licence | Why |
|---|---|---|

| PyInstaller bootloader | GPL-2.0 with a bootloader exception that permits distributing the built application under any licence | packaging |

(`unified-workstation-<version>.sbom.cdx.json`), generated by the release workflow, which also
fails on a licence that is incompatible with bundling.
